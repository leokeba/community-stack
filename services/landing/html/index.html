About This Infrastructure

This portal operates on the Community Communications Stack, an open-source, self-hosted infrastructure layer combining Matrix Synapse, Element Web, Caddy, and independent announcement distribution channels.

  • Independence: Operates independently of commercial corporate messaging platforms.
  • Resilience: Public updates remain readable even during external outages.
  • Separation: Public news, community rooms, and internal coordination remain distinct.
